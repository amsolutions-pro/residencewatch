Are there quotas for Armenian residence permits from 1 November 2026?

Yes: annual quotas per type of permit are introduced on 1 November 2026, but the figures are not published yet.

General information, not legal advice. Implementing rules, quotas and fees may still change. ResidenceWatch is not affiliated with the Government of Armenia.

What we know

  • Annual quotas are introduced per type of residence permit from 1 November 2026.
  • Once the quota for a category is reached, new applications in that category are refused.
  • Applications filed before 1 November are still assessed under the rules in force when they were filed.
  • The quota figures per category were not published when this page was last checked (22 October 2026). Check the quota for your category at the moment you apply.
