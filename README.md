# ResidenceWatch

Independent, dated and sourced information on residence rules in Armenia (reform of 1 November 2026): guide in English, Russian and Armenian, printable checklist, surname spelling tool.

- Pages: `index.html` (EN), `ru.html`, `hy.html`, `checklist.html`, `names.html`, `legal.html`, `privacy.html`, `changes.html`.
- No cookies, no analytics, no third-party content (fonts and the transliteration library are served from this repository).
- Finalise before publishing: `python finaliser.py <site-url> <contact-email>`.
- Tests of the surname tool: `node tests/test_names.cjs <path>/names.js <path>/vendor/armenian-transliteration-3.0.0.global.js`.
- General information, not legal advice. Not affiliated with the Government of Armenia.
