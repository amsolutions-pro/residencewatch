How much does an Armenian residence permit cost in 2026?

On the official pages on 22 October 2026: AMD 105,000 for temporary residence and AMD 140,000 for permanent residence. Higher amounts have been announced but are not confirmed on official pages.

General information, not legal advice. Implementing rules, quotas and fees may still change. ResidenceWatch is not affiliated with the Government of Armenia.

What we know

  • Temporary residence (up to one year): AMD 105,000, the same amount to extend it, according to the official pages on 22 October 2026.
  • Permanent residence (5 years): AMD 140,000, according to the official pages on 22 October 2026.
  • Temporary residence is free for some applicants, including minors and close relatives of an Armenian citizen.
  • Not confirmed: Repat Armenia gives AMD 155,950 (temporary) and 255,950 (permanent) with the 1 November changes, non-refundable if the application is rejected; other sources give about 150,000 and 250,000 from 1 January 2027. Amounts may change in 2027.
