What are the minimum thresholds for entrepreneurs seeking Armenian residence in 2026?

AMD 2,000,000 in a company's charter capital or in shares, or AMD 1,000,000 for an individual entrepreneur (bank balance or 60-day turnover), as reported for Article 29.4 of the Law on Foreigners.

What we know

  • Participant in a company: AMD 2,000,000 contributed to charter capital.
  • Owner of shares or securities: AMD 2,000,000 in shares or securities.
  • Individual entrepreneur, by bank balance: AMD 1,000,000 across bank accounts.
  • Individual entrepreneur, by activity: AMD 1,000,000 turnover in the 60 days before applying.
  • The status can be terminated if, within 180 days of being granted, the tax database shows no tax from the business activity, or if the invested capital falls below AMD 1,000,000 or is withdrawn.
  • These thresholds come from Article 29.4 of the Law "On Foreigners", added by Law HO-11-N of 20 January 2026, as reported by legal news sources; the official text was not available online when this page was last checked.
