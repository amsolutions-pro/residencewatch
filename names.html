How else might your Armenian surname be written?

Records spell the same family name in many ways: Eastern or Western Armenian, English, French, German or Russian usage, -yan or -ian, -iants or -yantz. Type a surname to get the spellings worth searching for in archives, registries and old documents.

    Suggestions, not proof. These spellings are generated from transliteration standards and common spelling habits. They help you search; they do not prove a family link. Armenian-script input gives the most precise results.

    Looking for your family's village?

    Place names changed too. A village remembered by its Armenian name was often renamed, so it has to be traced through its Armenian, Ottoman and present-day Turkish names. Two public resources help: Nişanyan's inventory of settlements in Turkey, which records old and new place names, and Houshamadyan, which reconstructs the life of Ottoman Armenian towns and villages from survivors' memoirs.
