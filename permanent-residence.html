How do you get permanent residence in Armenia after 1 November 2026?

Generally after three years of temporary status within the last five years, on one of five grounds; the 10-year special status is abolished.

General information, not legal advice. Implementing rules, quotas and fees may still change. ResidenceWatch is not affiliated with the Government of Armenia.

What we know

  • Step 1: a temporary residence permit, up to one year, with no extension: at expiry you file a new application.
  • Step 2: three years of temporary status within the last five, required for the family and entrepreneur grounds; the years do not need to be in a row.
  • Step 3: a permanent residence permit, generally for five years, on one of five grounds: family ties with an Armenian citizen, Armenian origin, entrepreneurial activity, investment, or exceptional services to Armenia.
  • The 10-year special status and special passport are abolished; people who already hold them keep their rights until expiry. Armenian origin remains one of the five grounds.
  • Unconfirmed: one law firm describes an investor fast track giving five years straight away; the investor rules (amount, eligible assets, holding period) were not published when this page was last checked.
