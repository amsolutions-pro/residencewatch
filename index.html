Armenia's residence rules change on 1 November 2026

What we know, what is still unknown, and what to prepare now. Every point is dated and sourced, and we say so when sources disagree.

Transition period. The new rules apply from 1 November 2026; implementing regulations, quotas and fees may still change. Every point below was last checked on 22 October 2026. General information, not legal advice. ResidenceWatch is not affiliated with the Government of Armenia or its Migration and Citizenship Service.

What changes on 1 November

  1. Applications move fully online to a new platform of the Migration and Citizenship Service. The current platform (migration.e-gov.am) and the EarlyOne app stop working on that date.
  2. The 10-year special residence status ends. Statuses already granted remain valid.
  3. Annual quotas are introduced per type of permit. Once a quota is reached, new applications in that category are refused. The figures are not published yet.
  4. Applications filed before 1 November are still assessed under the rules in force when they were filed.
  5. Extensions are no longer available. When a temporary permit expires, you must file a new application.
  6. Decisions within 30 days of a complete application.
  7. Absences of 183 days or more must be reported within 10 days.

The new path to permanent residence

1. Temporary residence permitUp to one year. No extension: at expiry you file a new application.
2. Three years of temporary status within the last fiveRequired for the family and entrepreneur grounds. The years do not need to be in a row.
3. Permanent residence permitGenerally five years, on one of five grounds: family ties with an Armenian citizen, Armenian origin, entrepreneurial activity, investment, or exceptional services to Armenia.

The 10-year special status and special passport are abolished. People who already hold them keep their rights until expiry. Armenian origin remains one of the five grounds for permanent residence.

Unconfirmed One law firm describes an investor fast track giving five years straight away. The government has not yet set the investor rules (amount, eligible assets, holding period). Do not invest for residence purposes until they are published.

Minimum thresholds for entrepreneurs

Article 29.4 of the Law "On Foreigners", added by Law HO-11-N of 20 January 2026, as reported by legal news sources; we will link the official text as soon as it is available online.

SituationMinimum
Participant in a companyAMD 2,000,000 contributed to charter capital
Owner of shares or securitiesAMD 2,000,000 in shares or securities
Individual entrepreneur, by bank balanceAMD 1,000,000 across bank accounts
Individual entrepreneur, by activityAMD 1,000,000 turnover in the 60 days before applying

The status can be terminated if, within 180 days of being granted, the tax database shows no tax from the business activity, or if the invested capital falls below AMD 1,000,000 or is withdrawn.

What to do now

  • Start with your documents. Certified translations and apostilles take two to eight weeks depending on the country. An incomplete online file can be rejected.
  • Ask whether you can still file before 1 November under the current rules. Appointments have been scarce since June.
  • Entrepreneurs: open a business account early and keep a record of your turnover for the last 60 days.
  • Check the quota for your category at the moment you apply, once figures are published.
  • Talk to a lawyer about your own case before paying fees or investing.
